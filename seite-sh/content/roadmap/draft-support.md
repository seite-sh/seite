Content with `draft: true` in frontmatter is excluded from production builds. The `--drafts` flag on `seite build` and `seite serve` includes draft content for local preview. [Scheduled publishing](/roadmap/scheduled-publishing) (future `date` filtering) shipped in v0.21.0.
