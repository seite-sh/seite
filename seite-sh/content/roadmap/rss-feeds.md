RSS 2.0 feed generation for collections with `has_rss: true`. Per-language feeds at `/{lang}/feed.xml`. Each item includes title, link, description, pubDate, and guid. RSS autodiscovery `<link>` tags in all theme `<head>` blocks. Atom format support tracked separately as a planned addition.
