Headings are automatically parsed during markdown rendering to produce a table of contents with `level`, `text`, and `id` fields. Slugified `id` attributes are injected into heading HTML for anchor linking. Available in templates as `page.toc` — an array of entries that themes can render as a sidebar nav, floating TOC, or in-page outline.
