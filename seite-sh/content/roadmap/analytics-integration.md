Five analytics providers supported via `[analytics]` in `seite.toml`: Google Analytics (GA4), Google Tag Manager, Plausible, Fathom, and Umami. Optional cookie consent banner with accessible ARIA labels and responsive CSS. Plausible extensions (tagged-events, outbound-links, file-downloads) and custom script URLs for self-hosted instances. GTM includes noscript fallback.
